<p>ARTopia, the Applied Learning Programme (ALP) in OPSS, exposes Orchidians to various genres of Art, Music, Dance, and Drama. Through the programme, we ignite students' passion in the Arts and nurture their 21st century competencies through hands-on, real-world learning experiences. Our students can look forward to regular workshops, performances both in and outside of school, and participating in exhibitions and competitions. </p>

<p>In addition to our ALP, our school also runs the Enhanced Art Programme (EAP), an upper secondary Art programme that allows students with interest and potential in Art to pursue the subject at a higher level.</p>
